About Pigs for Noobs!

If you're here, you've probably already gotten yourself a guinea pig or you're thinking about it.

Guinea pigs can be awesome, loving, and terrifically fun pets. But they require care and responsibility.

This website introduces guinea pig behavior, proper care, and useful resources for new owners.
